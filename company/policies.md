# Billing & policy reference

## Trial
14 days from signup, card required upfront, converts automatically unless cancelled before day 14.

## Annual plan
2 months free (effectively 10 months billed for 12) if switched before the trial converts. This is the only annual discount that exists — never offer a different percentage or dollar amount, and never stack it with anything else.

## Trial extension
One 7-day extension per customer, ever. Applied instantly, no approval needed, no reason required beyond what CLAUDE.md's offer logic already specifies (0-workout trial_dormant customers). Do not offer a second extension to someone who already got one — check `conversations/{email}.md` for a prior extension before offering.

## Cancellation
Self-serve in-app under Account > Subscription, effective at the end of the current billing period. No cancellation fee. We do not process cancellations via email — if a customer asks to cancel by replying to an email, tell them how to do it in-app, or offer to do it for them and confirm once done.

## Refunds
Full refund available within 14 days of any charge, no reason required. Refund requests are an escalation per CLAUDE.md — acknowledge once, don't process it inline over email, log it.

## Discounts outside the annual offer
None exist. Don't invent one, don't negotiate one, regardless of how the conversation is going.
