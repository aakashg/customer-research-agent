# Programs reference

Background detail for replying to customers accurately. Don't dump this wholesale into an email — pull in only what's relevant to what they asked.

## HIIT Express
20-30 min interval sessions, bodyweight + optional dumbbells. 4x/week suggested cadence. Good fit for people who feel short on time. Level range: beginner-intermediate.

## Mobility Daily
10-15 min daily sessions, no equipment. Focused on joint range of motion and recovery, not strength or cardio load. Good fit alongside another program or on its own for people returning from injury (once cleared) or easing into a routine. All levels.

## 5K Builder
3 runs/week plus one optional cross-training day, 8-week arc building to a 5K. Treadmill or outdoor, pace-based not distance-based early on. Level range: true beginner through returning runner.

## Strength Foundations
3x/week full-body sessions, 40-45 min, requires access to dumbbells or a light barbell setup (home or gym). Progressive overload built into the plan week over week. Level range: beginner-intermediate.

## Switching programs
Any customer can switch programs at any time with no penalty and no reset of billing. Their workout history stays attached to their account across the switch.
